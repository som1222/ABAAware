About ABAAware

Improving antibiotic understanding through education.

ABAAware is an educational initiative focused on improving antibiotic literacy and helping communities better understand when antibiotics are useful, when they are not, and why responsible use matters.

Our goal is to make reliable antibiotic information easier to understand through educational resources, outreach, and community engagement.

Antibiotics 101

What exactly are antibiotics?

Antibiotics are medicines used to treat certain infections caused by bacteria. Different antibiotics work in different ways, such as interfering with bacterial growth or helping kill bacteria.

01

Bacteria

Antibiotics are designed to target bacteria, although the correct treatment depends on the specific infection.

02

Viruses

Viral illnesses such as colds and flu do not respond to antibiotics.

03

Medical Guidance

A healthcare professional can determine whether an antibiotic is appropriate for a particular illness.

Antibiotic Resistance

The bacteria become resistant — not your body.

Antibiotic resistance occurs when bacteria change over time and antibiotics become less effective against them.

Resistant infections may become harder to treat and can require different medicines or longer treatment.

Responsible antibiotic use is one important part of protecting the effectiveness of these medicines.

Use Antibiotics Responsibly

Simple habits can make a difference.

1

Use antibiotics only when prescribed.

Antibiotics should be used based on advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

2

Follow the directions you receive.

Take the medication according to the instructions provided by your healthcare professional or pharmacist.

3

Do not share antibiotics.

A medication prescribed for one person or infection may not be appropriate for another.

4

Do not self-treat with leftover medication.

If you become sick again, speak with a healthcare professional instead of using antibiotics left from a previous illness.

Myths vs. Facts

How much do you really know about antibiotics?

MYTH

“Antibiotics can help treat a cold.”

FACT

Most common colds are caused by viruses, and antibiotics do not work against viruses.

MYTH

“Antibiotic resistance means your body has become resistant.”

FACT

Resistance occurs in bacteria. The bacteria change in ways that reduce the effectiveness of antibiotics.

MYTH

“It is okay to use someone else's antibiotics if you have similar symptoms.”

FACT

Similar symptoms can have different causes, and antibiotics should only be taken when appropriately prescribed.
